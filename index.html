BEFORE WE BEGIN

The details
make the difference.

What should I include in a roller enquiry?+

Include your press manufacturer and model, roller position, core and finished dimensions, ink type, and any known hardness requirements. A drawing or an existing roller reference can help clarify the scope.

Which printing and industrial applications are covered?+

The Meiwa portfolio includes rollers for offset, gravure, flexo, newspaper, and label printing. Industrial ranges cover film processing, paper manufacturing, plywood, and steel lines. Share your application so the suitable range and availability can be discussed.

How do I select the right rubber material?+

Start with your ink and cleaning chemistry, press conditions, and roller’s function. Share these details in your enquiry so the material requirements can be discussed before a specification is agreed.

Are Meiwa-technology rollers already manufactured in India?+

Local manufacturing is being explored. Discuss production arrangements, supply timing, and technical support with NUTECH for your requirement.
