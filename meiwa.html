03 — WHY MEIWA

The expertise
behind the materials.

  1. 01

    80 years of compounding expertise

    Material science built since 1946 informs proprietary rubber compounds for demanding printing and industrial environments.

  2. 02

    Continuous research & development

    A dedicated technology-development team works on materials for new inks, faster presses and changing film substrates.

  3. 03

    A documented quality history

    Corporate ISO 9001 certification and historical plant-level ISO 9002 milestones form part of Meiwa’s quality-management story.

  4. 04

    Experience across print & film

    A full-line portfolio serves offset, gravure, flexo, newspaper and film-processing operations, drawing on decades of industry experience.
