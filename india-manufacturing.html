NUTECH / INDIA MANUFACTURING

Japanese material science.
An Indian ambition.

NUTECH and Meiwa are exploring local manufacturing of Meiwa-technology rubber rollers in India—connecting eight decades of Japanese material expertise with India’s growing print and packaging market.

The proposal ↓ Four objectives ↓ Planned visit ↓ Meeting & product enquiries ↓

01 — THE MANUFACTURING DISCUSSION

A collaboration.
A considered next step.

From expertise to local production

NUTECH’s confirmed technical collaboration with Meiwa forms the foundation for the manufacturing discussion. Local production is being explored; production arrangements, supply timing and the scope of support will be confirmed as the proposal develops.

NUTECH is a separate company established by the founders of Teams Equipment LLP. Their continuing machinery business imports used offset printing machinery and graphic equipment from the USA and Europe, alongside UV systems, die cutters and film laminators from China.

Understanding a customer’s production requirement remains the shared starting point.

LOCAL MANUFACTURING / UNDER EXPLORATION

02 — FOUR PROPOSED BENEFITS

Local access.
A long-term perspective.

These objectives describe the proposed model. They are ambitions for the local-manufacturing opportunity, with details to be agreed.

  1. 01

    Local manufacturing

    Meiwa-technology rollers made in India, aimed at faster delivery and local stock availability.

  2. 02

    Japanese quality, local pricing

    Meiwa’s engineering experience paired with a cost structure suited to the Indian market.

  3. 03

    Technical support & training

    Proposed direct technology transfer, training and after-sales support drawing on Meiwa’s research and development expertise.

  4. 04

    Make in India, backed by Japan

    The ambition to become a long-term supply partner for India’s offset, flexo, gravure and film industries.
