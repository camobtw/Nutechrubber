04 — ANTI-STATIC ROLLS

Static control.
Ozone resistance.

In film and paper transport lines, friction between rotating rubber rolls generates static electricity. Static can cause dust pickup and wrinkles, and ozone can crack the roll surface. Meiwa’s anti-static range lowers surface electrical resistance across named material grades.
